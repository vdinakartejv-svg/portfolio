About me

This is Dinakar Tej, and I am an Animation and Multimedia student pursuing a Bachelor of Visual Arts (BVA) at Koshys Group of Institutions.

I have a strong interest in 3D Modelling and Digital Art, with a passion for creating detailed, visually appealing and professionally presented 3D models. I enjoy exploring different modelling techniques, understanding forms and structures, and developing my technical abilities through practical projects.

My areas of interest include 3D modelling, hard-surface modelling, organic modelling, digital sculpting, UV mapping, texturing, material creation, lighting and rendering.

CAREER OBJECTIVE

My career objective is to establish myself as a skilled and professional 3D Artist and 3D Modeller in the creative industry.
